SCENE: INT. THE BALL-SACK HOUSE — LATE AFTERNOON

[NARRATOR]
The doorbell of the Ball-Sack house hasn't worked since the Carter administration, so Clark knocks. And when Hunter opens the door, he starts to say something, but stops.
